# Focusin Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 4, 2026

Focusin is a local-only macOS application. It does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or network functionality.

## Data handled by Focusin

Focusin stores the following information locally on your Mac using Apple-provided preferences storage:

- Focus and break settings.
- Current timer state and deadlines.
- Same-day identifiers for activities shown, completed, or skipped.
- Notification and launch-at-login preferences.

This information is used only to provide the application’s focus/break workflow and restore it after relaunch. Focusin does not transmit this information to the developer or a third party.

## System integrations

Focusin can optionally request notification permission and register itself as a login item. macOS controls these permissions, and you can change them in System Settings. Focusin remains usable when notifications are denied.

The static beta-feedback template is maintained as an internal beta-preparation resource. Focusin does not copy to or read from the clipboard.

## Data collection and tracking

Focusin does not collect personal data, track users, sell data, or use third-party SDKs. Its App Sandbox configuration has no network entitlement.

## Wellness disclaimer

Focusin provides optional general-wellness prompts, not medical advice. You can skip any activity that is unsuitable for you or your surroundings.

## Changes

If Focusin’s data practices change, this policy and the application’s privacy manifest will be updated before the changed version is distributed.

## Contact

The public support contact is not yet assigned. Focusin must not be publicly distributed until a working contact path is published here and in its store listing.
