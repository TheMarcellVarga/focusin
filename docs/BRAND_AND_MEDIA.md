# Brand And Media

## Brand Position

Focusin is a practical focus companion, not a clinical wellness service or high-pressure productivity system. The brand should communicate calm momentum, privacy, and small useful actions.

## Name And Descriptor

- Product name: Focusin.
- Preferred descriptor: “State-aware micro-breaks for your Mac.”
- Short listing line: “A private menu bar timer that suggests the right short reset.”

Avoid suggesting medical outcomes, employee monitoring, or AI personalization.

## Voice

- Direct: “Take a two-minute reset.”
- Respectful: skipping has no penalty or shame.
- Specific: name the action and duration.
- Calm: avoid urgency unless the runtime state requires attention.
- Honest: say local, private, and no account only while those claims remain true.

## Visual Direction

- Use a simple figure/mind/body or focus-reset motif that remains recognizable at menu bar and 16-point sizes.
- Prefer restrained system-adjacent color over gradients and wellness clichés.
- App icon must work in macOS rounded-square presentation and preserve a distinct silhouette.
- Menu bar symbol should be monochrome/template-compatible.
- Marketing images should show the real app, not conceptual dashboards.

## Required Assets

- Production app icon set at all macOS sizes.
- Menu bar/SF Symbol mapping for four runtime states.
- App Store screenshots showing idle/focus, break due, active break, and Settings.
- Optional short preview video following the demo walkthrough.
- Repository/social preview and portfolio hero image.

## Screenshot Plan

1. Hero: popover with a clear state-aware recommendation.
2. Focus cycle: menu bar countdown and status panel.
3. Break cycle: activity instructions, progress, and remaining time.
4. Control: reminder, quiet-hour, and recommendation settings.
5. Privacy: local-only positioning paired with the actual System tab or concise graphic.

Screenshots must use plausible content, hide unrelated personal menu bar items where appropriate, and match the submitted build.

## App Store Copy Guardrails

- Allowed: local-only, no account, works offline, state-aware, low-intensity micro-breaks.
- Avoid: treats anxiety/pain, prevents injury, scientifically proven, boosts productivity by a quantified amount, or guarantees focus.
- Clearly state macOS version requirements and notification optionality.

## Asset Acceptance Criteria

- Legible at required resolutions and in light/dark contexts.
- No placeholder icon, stock watermark, or mismatched UI.
- Screenshot copy passes privacy and wellness review.
- Assets are source-controlled or their reproducible source is documented.
- App Store, README, TestFlight, and portfolio presentation use consistent naming and claims.
